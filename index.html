Common questions

Does it need internet?

No. Everything works offline. The internet is only used when you share a PDF or slip through WhatsApp or SMS.

Does the EMI cut salary automatically?

No. A planned EMI is only recorded. Open the employee’s Payroll tab and tap Commit Monthly EMI for the month you want to deduct. The slip and PDF then update.

Can Net Payable go below zero?

No. Net Payable never drops below ₹0, and EMI is capped at the loan balance and at earned salary plus overtime.

How do I move to a new phone?

Export a JSON backup in Settings, then import it on the new phone. Keep “Replace all existing data” off to merge, or on for a clean restore.

How do I install the APK?

Download the file and open it. If Android asks, allow “Install unknown apps” for your browser, then tap Install.
